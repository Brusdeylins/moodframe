# Shot list: images for moodframe.art

Everything is made in MoodFrame itself, in the empty library (`~/.moodframe`, the real one is set aside), UI in
English. Only standard moods and frames are used, so every visitor finds them in the app. The library is at the
same time the content of the overview screenshot.

Order: first the chats, then the screenshots. The number of images per entry is up to you; see "Screenshots"
for what looks best.

## Chat 1: "The red coat" (selected in the overview, source of the Mood chapter)

One prompt, five standard moods. Frame **Social Post (4:5, 2K)**.

Prompt (the same for all five entries):

> A young woman in a long red wool coat stands at a wet city crossing at the end of the day, looking back over
> her shoulder, the street behind her softly out of focus, puddles reflecting the lights

| # | Mood | Model | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vintage Film | Nano Banana Pro | warm golden hour, analog grain |
| 2 | Quiet Morning | Nano Banana Pro | blue hour, calm, cool palette |
| 3 | Sci-Fi Cinema | FLUX.2 [pro] | neon practicals, night, magenta and cyan |
| 4 | Film Noir | Nano Banana Pro | black and white, hard shadows (the red coat turns grey: the mood wins) |
| 5 | Dreamy Pastel | FLUX.2 [pro] | watercolor, high key: the style changes too |

Website: one image per mood for the tabs in the Mood chapter (the best one).

## Screenshots (Retina, window about 1440 × 900)

What looks best:

- Chat view: blocks with 4 images show the choice an entry offers; mix in a block with 1 or 2 images.
- Images view: neighbours should differ, so every image (or every second one) shows another mood or scene.
- Tip: for 4 different images in one entry, FLUX.2 gives real variations (a seed per image); Nano Banana
  returns nearly the same image for identical requests.

1. Overview: chat view of Chat 1, list "By chat" with all chats, right panel open (Mood, Frame, Generation).
2. Optional: images view of Chat 1 (task 46), for a second overview picture.
